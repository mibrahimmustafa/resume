Biography

Mohamed Abdelrahman is a Senior System Architect and Technical Team Leader with over 17 years of progressive experience in system architecture, multi-cloud engineering, DevOps automation, AI/ML operations, and enterprise CRM transformation. Based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, he has led cross-functional technical teams and delivered large-scale infrastructure, platform, and automation initiatives across healthcare, telecommunications, and enterprise IT environments in both Egypt and KSA.

Throughout his career, Mohamed has operated at the intersection of architecture, operations, and business strategy — designing resilient multi-cloud platforms, automating operational toil through AI-powered workflows and CI/CD pipelines, and modernising legacy systems into scalable, containerised architectures. He holds an MSc in Computer Science and AI, a Dual MBA in General Management, and a B.Sc. in Electronics and Communications Engineering, complemented by 12+ professional certifications across AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft, PMP, ITIL, and cybersecurity.

Mohamed is known for a hands-on leadership style that balances technical depth with business awareness: he architects solutions that are operationally sustainable, cost-aware, and aligned with organisational goals, while mentoring teams to raise the overall engineering standard. He is equally comfortable leading a cloud migration, troubleshooting a production incident, building an automation framework from scratch, or presenting architectural decisions to stakeholders.

Core Values & Working Principles

Team Enablement

Automation First

Business-Aligned Technology

Technical decisions are evaluated against reliability, cost, time-to-value, and the organisation's strategic direction — not in isolation.
