AI-Powered Automation Framework

2024 · SCFHS, Riyadh

Designed and deployed an AI-powered automation framework that integrates N8N workflows, LangChain-based agents, and LLM-driven triage to reduce manual triage effort across multiple service channels. The framework evaluates incoming requests, routes them to the appropriate queue or resolver, and provides auto-suggested responses where confident — keeping a human-in-the-loop for edge cases.

N8N LangChain LLM AI Agents Automation Triage Workflow Orchestration

Outcomes

  • ~60% reduction in manual triage time across three service channels.
  • ~29% reduction in overall operational toil through automation of repetitive triage and routing tasks.
  • Human-in-the-loop design maintained quality and safety for sensitive cases.
  • Established a reusable automation pattern that can be extended to additional channels and use cases.
